Connecting from the hosted site?

Vercel hosts the instrument, not the OSC bridge. In a local checkout on your computer, start (or restart) the bridge with:

npm run bridge

127.0.0.1 means this device. Allow local-network access if your browser asks. If HTTPS blocks the local connection, run the instrument locally, or use a trusted TLS bridge at a wss:// address. Changing ws:// to wss:// alone does not enable TLS.

Browser sound and external out are independent. Silence the browser and let your own instruments speak.

FIELD NOTE 001 — THE WALL

specimen sheet · obs. 480×640 px · n = 4–8

BUGSONIC

Small creatures.
Living surfaces.
Unstable sounds.

A wall is never silent.

Beneath its paint, its cracks and its scars, there is a landscape waiting to be explored.

We introduce small digital creatures into this landscape. They crawl, hesitate, collide, and listen. They read the surface through edges, shadows, textures, and fractures.

Every encounter leaves a trace.

A crack becomes a click.
An edge becomes a note.
A vibration changes the terrain.

The creatures listen to what they disturb. The surface remembers what they do. Sound becomes movement. Movement becomes sound.

There is no fixed composition. No final image. Only a living system, unfolding in real time.

Observe. Disturb. Listen. Repeat.

The wall is your instrument.

— BUGSONIC / THE WALL
